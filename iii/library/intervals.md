# intervals

_grid to MIDI note map_

made by [tehn](https://nnnnnnnn.co)

## Download

- [intervals.lua](https://codeberg.org/tehn/iii-scripts/raw/branch/main/grid/intervals.lua)

## Instructions

Play keys to send MIDI notes. C octaves are lit brightly, other white keys dimly.

Left column is a slider specifying the velocity, low to high.

Bottom left key when held lets you select the MIDI channel (left to right, 1-16).
